--- title: Tools layout: default ---

Small, self-contained utilities that run entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded — the heavy lifting happens locally via WebAssembly, so your files never leave your machine.

WebAssembly

GIF Maker

Turn a video into a high-quality animated GIF, all in your browser.

 WebCodecs

Video Compressor

Shrink a video to a target size or bitrate using your machine's hardware encoder — H.264 or H.265, in your browser.

 Offline

PDF Signer

Stamp a picture of your signature anywhere on a PDF — one page or every page — add text and dates, and save it. The file never leaves your machine.
